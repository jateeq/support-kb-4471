How each report is placed

A report is placed by its tenant and the way it came in, and by what its body says. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, so this page does not use it.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and these tenants write the day first. Day and month swap: a booking for 7 April goes out as 4 July. It covers app and email reports from every day-first tenant, and form reports from tenants on booking-v2, whose hint already says DD/MM/YYYY.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Customers of a day-first tenant follow the hint, and the form reads what they typed the tenant’s way. This covers reports that came in through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it, because the form receives the wrong input.
  • clock: the booking moves by exactly one day, with day and month in the right place. The desk read these bodies as a one-day move and listed them in its notes. One customer in Lisbon and one in Toronto saw different days for the same meeting, so this is a time zone problem. The desk filed these reports under parser by tenant, and the parser fix does not touch them either.

What to do on the desk

  • When a body describes a one-day move, add its id to the desk notes, so the table above counts it under clock.
  • Keep #4471 open until the tickets for all three problems are closed.
